# phd-trp

Thesis Research Plan (TRP) of the PhD thesis proposal
_RicoSLAM: Long-Term Localization and Mapping in Dynamic Environments_.
This proposal was submitted on December 20, 2022, to the Scientific Committee of
the Doctoral Program in Electrical and Computers Engineering
([PDEEC](https://paginas.fe.up.pt/~pdeec/))
at the
[Faculty of Engineering, University of Porto (FEUP)](https://sigarra.up.pt/feup/en/WEB_PAGE.INICIAL).
The presentation and discussion of this TRP was on January 12, 2022, and had the
following participants:
- Prof. Luis Miguel Pinho de Almeida (PDEEC Scientific Committee)
- Supervisor: Prof. António Paulo Gomes Mendes Moreira (FEUP/DEEC)
- Second supervisor: Dr. Héber Miguel Sobreira (INESC TEC)
- FEUP member: Prof. Paulo José Cerqueira Gomes da Costa
- External member: Dr. Rob Deaves (Dyson, UK)
